    Smart money
    Smart Money Index: the first 30 minutes are mostly emotional retail and overnight orders; the last hour is mostly institutions. SMI = last-hour move − first-30-min move, summed over days. Large prints are single trades of $250k or more in SOXL (bull) or SOXS (bear), signed by the tick rule. No free source shows institutional orders directly; these are proxies.

    Rule finder · which price-action links hold up, and who causes them
    Method: each link is tested on the first 70% of stored days (in sample), then checked on the last 30% (out of sample, days that the first test did not use), and in 4 separate time blocks. Reliable = significant in sample (|t| ≥ 2), same direction out of sample (|t| ≥ 1.5), and the same sign in at least 3 of 4 blocks. Likely = significant and consistent, but weaker out of sample. Fragile = significant once but not stable, so probably luck. Hit rate = how often the rule called the direction correctly out of sample. With 15 tests, about 1 can pass in sample by chance, so the out-of-sample check is the important one.

    The memory bank is in this browser (IndexedDB). It stays after you close the tab or restart. It learns only during regular market hours while this page is open. Only one tab learns at a time. Use Export backup to keep a copy or to move the memory to a different device.
    Weights use share counts from the iShares SOXX holdings file (Oct 19, 2026). SOXX tracks the same index as SOXL. Weight = shares × previous close. Update the HOLDINGS list after each quarterly rebalance.
