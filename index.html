Caller tracker · who calls $SOXL right on StockTwits
Each StockTwits post tagged Bullish or Bearish is a call. The page checks it against SOXL 1 hour later and at the next 4:00 pm close (moves under 0.3% do not count; repeat posts in 30 min count once). Score = the lowest win rate that is still likely after this many calls, so a lucky 3/3 does not beat a steady 30/45. A poster needs 6 scored calls. The record stays in this browser and grows every day. Not advice; past calls do not promise future calls.

    Each block shows: what already happened (actual), the historical average for that time block, and what the validated rules expect. Only rules marked Reliable or Likely in the rule finder are used.
    Rule finder · which price-action links hold up, and who causes them
    Method: each link is tested on the first 70% of stored days (in sample), then checked on the last 30% (out of sample, days that the first test did not use), and in 4 separate time blocks. Reliable = significant in sample (|t| ≥ 2), same direction out of sample (|t| ≥ 1.5), and the same sign in at least 3 of 4 blocks. Likely = significant and consistent, but weaker out of sample. Fragile = significant once but not stable, so probably luck. Hit rate = how often the rule called the direction correctly out of sample. With 15 tests, about 1 can pass in sample by chance, so the out-of-sample check is the important one.
